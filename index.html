Difficulty
Cartoon-style zombie action with loud sounds. An original game, not connected to any other game.

How to play

Goal

Survive as many waves as you can. Every wave has more zombies, and they get faster and tougher. Brutes are huge and tanky.

Points

Hits and kills earn points. Headshots earn the most and do double damage.

Weapons

You start with a pistol. Walk up to the glowing signs by the barn and farmhouse and press E (or BUY) to get the shotgun or rifle. Buy the same sign again for ammo. The ammo crate in the middle refills your current gun.

Staying alive

Keep moving, circle the buildings and never get cornered. Your health slowly comes back if you avoid damage. Pick up green crosses and ammo boxes that zombies drop.
