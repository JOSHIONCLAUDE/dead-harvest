Dead Harvest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Dead Harvest is a free, single-player zombie survival game. We collect nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no trackers. The game works completely offline.

What stays on your device

Your settings and best wave score (look speed, volume, brightness, graphics quality and difficulty) are saved only on your own device. We never receive them. Clearing the app's data deletes them.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information.
  • We do not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • The game does not connect to the internet while you play.

Scary content

This game contains cartoon-style zombie action and loud sounds. It is an original game and is not made by or connected to any other game or company.

Children

The game collects no personal information at all. Because it has zombie action, we suggest it for older children and up, and that a parent decides what is right for their child.
